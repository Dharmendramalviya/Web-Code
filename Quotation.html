A rose by any other name would smell as sweet

A rose by any other name would smell as sweet" is a famous line from William Shakespeare's play Romeo and Juliet. It means that the essence or nature of something is not changed by its name or label. Juliet uses this line to argue that Romeo's identity as a Montague, his family name, shouldn't matter in their love for each other.
